▦ Tilemaps

Paint compact 32 × 32 px terrain with independent artwork, collision, animation, and Mechanics behavior.

Tilemaps are sparse cell grids attached to named draw layers. Cells do not become separate map objects, so large painted areas remain compact and are edited with the Tilemap Brush.

Paint a tilemap

  1. Open a map in the editor and choose Add → Tilemap Brush.
  2. Choose the draw layer, cell behavior, collision shape, and artwork in the placement toolbar.
  3. Click or drag on the canvas to paint. Use the editor's Erase tool to remove cells.
  4. Switch layers to paint separate terrain and foreground or background detail.

Cells align to the 32 px world grid. Their visual layer controls draw order and parallax; it does not change collision. The editor keeps the canvas and placement coordinates aligned, while configured parallax takes effect during Play and Test.

Cell behavior

BehaviorEffect
SolidBlocks the player. Use a full-cell box or a custom convex polygon.
One-way platformSupports the player from above and permits passage from below. A polygon can shape the surface.
Ramp rising to the right / leftUses a sloped collision surface so the player can walk up or down the tile.
Damage on touchHurts a player who touches the cell; it does not act as a platform.
DecorativeDraws artwork without collision or damage.

Artwork and collision are stored independently. Changing the selected atlas frame does not change the cell's behavior. Custom polygons use 3–12 ordered, convex vertices whose coordinates are normalized from 0 to 1 within the cell; this option applies to solid and one-way cells.

Tile atlases

Upload a PNG, JPEG, or WebP image for a layer, then set the frame width and height and select a frame index. Frames are read left to right and top to bottom. Atlas images are bounded to 8 MiB and 32 megapixels combined per map. Clear the atlas to return to built-in textures. The image and frame settings are saved with the map and travel in .pkrn exports.

Animation

Choose one of two animation modes when painting a cell:

  • Texture cycle rotates through 2–8 built-in texture frames.
  • Atlas frame cycle rotates through 2–8 selected frames from the layer's atlas.

Both modes support 1–12 frames per second. Animation runs in the editor, Play, and Test; each cell keeps its authored collision. A map can animate at most 2,048 cells.

Use tilemaps in Mechanics

  • Player Touches Tilemap runs when the player enters matching collidable cells.
  • Player Leaves Tilemap runs after contact with all matching cells ends and includes the last contacted cell. Contact can start again afterward.
  • Both triggers can filter by solid, one-way (including ramps), or damage behavior.
  • Set Tilemap Cell Behavior changes a selected or touched cell during the session.
  • Set Tilemap Cell Frame changes a cell's atlas animation frame without changing its collision.
  • Set Tilemap Cell Enabled removes a cell temporarily or restores its authored artwork and behavior.

Runtime changes reset to the saved map state when the session resets. Supported changes in hosted rooms are checked by the room host and synchronized to players.

Save, export, and performance

Tilemap cells, collision polygons, atlases, animation, draw-layer assignments, and Mechanics references persist through map save/load and .pkrn import/export. Cell data is stored sparsely rather than as one object per cell. Large-map streaming and unlimited animation are not supported.
