🟫 Block

Solid platforming surface — the core building unit of every Parkoreen map, now with texture support.

Overview

Blocks are 32×32 px objects the player can stand on, jump off, and push against. They are solid from every direction by default; a block can be set as a one-way platform so players land on its top while falling and pass through it from below. Ground blocks also support box, circular, capsule, two one-way ramps, and custom convex polygon collision shapes. Capsules round both ends along the block’s longer axis and collide from every side. Blocks can have a visual texture overlaid on top of their color.

Textures

Texture valueAppearance
nonePlain filled rectangle with bevel (default).
solidFlat, no bevel — good for seamless tiling.
brickBrick pattern drawn over the fill color.

Ground Collision ShapeBoxChoose a rectangular box, an inscribed circle, a capsule with rounded ends along the longer axis, a rising left-to-right/right-to-left ramp, or a custom convex polygon. Polygon vertices are ordered normalized [x, y] pairs from 0 to 1 (3–12 vertices); concave, self-intersecting, out-of-range, and degenerate shapes are rejected. Ramps are one-way surfaces. Ramp shapes are always one-way. Custom polygons default to one-way; disable One-Way Polygon Surface to collide with all polygon sides.

One-way platform controls

While standing on a one-way platform, press Down + Jump to drop through it. This consumes the ground jump as the player leaves the surface, with the normal short coyote-time grace period.

Rendering

Without a texture, blocks are drawn as filled rectangles with a 1 px bevel (lighter top/left edges, darker bottom/right). The bevel color is derived automatically from the block's fill color. With the solid texture the bevel is omitted for seamless edges. Collision geometry is independent from artwork; enable tester touchboxes to inspect circular, ramp, and custom polygon colliders. One-way platforms also have a warm highlight along the top so their collision direction is visible in the editor and during play.
